Key Acronyms & Terms – Quick Reference Table

Acronym / Term Meaning / Explanation
G (HG) Handgun – weapons that use the Handgun skill.
G (R/S) Rifle / Shotgun – weapons that use the Rifle/Shotgun skill (single‑shot rifles, shotguns, etc.).
G (SMG) Sub‑Machine Gun – weapons that use the Submachine skill (burst or full‑auto fire).
G (MG) Machine Gun – weapons that use the Machine Gun skill (full‑auto, heavy support).
Skill The specific skill required to operate the weapon (Handgun, Rifle/Shotgun, Submachine, or Machine Gun).
Damage Dice rolled to determine raw damage. On an Extreme success the damage is increased: • Non‑impaling weapons → maximum damage (plus max damage bonus, if any). • Impaling weapons → maximum damage + damage bonus and an extra weapon‑damage roll. Only the attacker’s initial attack can generate an Extreme‑damage boost; fighting‑back attacks never get the extra boost.
Impaling (i) Weapon category that can pierce vital areas on an Extreme success (e.g., blades, bullets, some shot‑gun slugs). Marked with (i) in tables.
Base Range Standard effective distance for the weapon’s damage tier.
Uses per Round Number of attacks the weapon can initiate in a combat round (does not affect the number of fighting‑back attacks).
Bullets in Gun (Magazine) Capacity of the magazine/drum. Some weapons list multiple capacities (e.g., “1 / 2” for double‑barrel).
Cost Price split by era (1920s vs. modern collector’s market). Shown as “$X / $Y” where applicable.
Common in Era Availability of the weapon in the listed historical period.
Malfunction (Mal) If the roll meets or exceeds the weapon’s malfunction number, the weapon fails to fire (not just a miss).
Rare Weapon is obsolete, collectible, or possibly illegal; often expensive or hard to obtain.
+DB “Plus Damage Bonus” – the weapon adds the user’s Damage Bonus (DB) to the damage roll (or max DB on Extreme).
Stun Target is incapacitated for 1D6 rounds (or as the Keeper decides).
Burn Target must roll Luck to avoid catching fire; takes minimum damage the next round, then damage doubles each subsequent round until extinguished (if flammable).
Burst / Full Auto Some firearms can switch to burst (typically 3‑round) or continuous fire. Use the automatic fire rules (penalty dice per extra shot).
1B / 2B 1 Barrel or 2 Barrels – number of barrels that can fire in a single round.
1/2 , 1/3 Weapon can be fired accurately every second or third round (e.g., bolt‑action rifles).
1 or 2 One or two barrels may fire in the same round (e.g., double‑barrel shotguns).
N/A Not applicable or not commercially available (e.g., auto‑fire configuration for a weapon that cannot fire that way).
Explosion Radius (yards) Damage radius for explosives (e.g., “2 yards”). Damage halves at double the radius, quarters at triple, and is ignored beyond that.
Assault Rifle Note Use Rifle/Shotgun skill for single shots; switch to Submachine skill for burst/full‑auto fire.
Chainsaw Note Extremely unwieldy: double the user’s chance of a fumble. A fumble deals 2D8 damage to the user; a malfunction stalls or jams the chain. A major wound may sever a random limb.
Claymore Mine Cone‑shaped 120° effect; damage follows the standard explosive radius rules.
Col. Moran’s Air Rifle Compressed‑air, silent operation; uses Rifle/Shotgun skill.
Dynamite / Hand Grenade Damage: 4D10 (≤ 3 yd), 2D10 (≤ 6 yd), 1D10 (≤ 9 yd).
Garrote Victim must make a Fighting Maneuver to escape; otherwise suffers 1D6 damage per round. Works only on humanoids or similar.
GE Mini‑Gun Gatling‑style heavy MG; typically mounted (requires Build 2+ to fire handheld).
Handguns (multi‑shot) Firing more than one shot per round adds one penalty die to each additional shot (max shots shown in brackets).
LAW Light Anti‑Tank Weapon; disposable, single‑shot.
Mace Spray No point‑blank bonus die. Target rolls ≤ (DEX ÷ 5) on 1D100 to avoid temporary blindness; effective only on humans or similar.
Rifles Most fire a single shot per round; reload time, not chambering, consumes the round. Assault rifles have a selector for single, 3‑round burst, or full‑auto.
Shotguns Three damage tiers (short/medium/long). Cannot impale unless loaded with slugs (see slug table). Extreme success at close range can deal up to 24 damage.
Shotgun Slugs 10‑gauge 1D10 + 7, 12‑gauge 1D10 + 6, 16‑gauge 1D10 + 5, 20‑gauge 1D10 + 4; base range 50 yd; impale capable.
Taser (Contact/Dart) Affects targets with Build ≤ 2; stunned for 1D6 rounds (or Keeper’s decision).

Use this table as a quick‑lookup when reading weapon stats or resolving combat in Call of Cthulhu.
