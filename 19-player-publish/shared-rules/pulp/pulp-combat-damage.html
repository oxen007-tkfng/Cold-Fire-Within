Pulp Combat Damage

Pulp Combat Damage

Category: combat

Faster Recovery

  • Natural Healing – +2 HP per day (Quick Healer talent bumps this to +3 HP/day).
  • First Aid / Medicine Boost – Successful rolls heal +1D4 HP (Extreme success = 4 HP).

Ignoring Major Wounds

  • No Major‑Wound Penalty – Heroes do not suffer the usual major‑wound effects.
  • Death Thresholds
    • Instant death: Single attack ≥ maximum HP.
    • Dying: Single attack ≥ ½ maximum HP and reduces HP to 0.
    • Unconscious: HP drops to 0 (but not ≥ ½ max).
    • Half‑HP Shock: If a single blow cuts HP by ≥ ½ max and leaves the hero above 0, they must make a CON roll to stay conscious.

Dying and Death

  • Dying State – Mark current HP as “D” (dying).
  • CON Rolls – Made at the end of each round; any failure = immediate death.
  • Stabilizing
    • First Aid (success) → removes “D”, restores 1D4 HP (Extreme = 4 HP).
    • Extreme Cases (e.g., being ripped apart) may render First Aid impossible – death is inevitable.
  • Follow‑up Medicine – After stabilization, a successful Medicine roll adds another 1D4 HP (Extreme = 4 HP).

Natural Healing After Near‑Death

  • Base Rate – +2 HP/day (or +3 HP/day with Quick Healer).
  • Medical Assistance – Each successful daily Medicine roll adds 1 HP to that day’s natural healing (total +3 HP).
  • Modifiers – Hospital care may grant a bonus die; unsanitary conditions may impose a penalty die.
  • Failed Medicine – May reduce healing (e.g., lose 1 HP or, on a pushed roll, lose 1D3 HP).

Sanity Impact

  • Near‑Death Trauma – Typically triggers a Sanity roll (0 or 1 SAN loss depending on outcome).
  • Role‑Playing Hook – Encourage players to describe lingering injuries (broken limbs, missing fingers, etc.) for dramatic effect, but remember pulp heroes can often “brush it off.”

Luck as a Death‑Avoidance Mechanic

  • Spending Luck – A dying hero may expend all remaining Luck points (minimum 30 LP required) to cheat death.
  • Result of Luck Spend
    • Luck drops to 0.
    • Hero immediately regains 1D6 + 1 HP.
    • Injuries remain; further healing still required.
    • No further CON rolls needed for that death episode.
  • Narrative Requirement – Player must describe how the hero miraculously survives.

Example Flow (Jack)

  1. Max HP = 22, current = 8.
  2. Trap deals 18 damage → HP = ‑10 → Dying (≥ ½ max, reduced to 0).
  3. First Aid succeeds → “D” removed, 1D4 HP restored (e.g., 3 HP).
  4. Medicine roll succeeds → additional 1D4 HP (e.g., 4 HP).
  5. Natural healing resumes (+2 HP/day, possibly +1 HP from daily Medicine).

Example Flow (Che “Holy‑Fire” Westner)

  1. Max HP = 34, current = 17.
  2. Star‑spawn claw deals 19 damage → HP = ‑2 → Dying (≥ ½ max, reduced to 0).
  3. Luck spend (≥ 30 LP) → Luck → 0, regain 1D6 + 1 HP (e.g., 5 HP).
  4. CON roll at end of round succeeds → remains conscious.
  5. Allies apply First Aid (success) → +1D4 HP (e.g., 3 HP).
  6. Daily Medicine rolls add extra HP each day (+1 HP per successful roll).

Example Flow (Maximillian Quest)

  • Facing instant death from Cthulhu’s foot, Maximillian spends all 36 Luck (≥ 30).
  • Describes a cinematic survival (e.g., diving behind a shattered column, a gust of wind lifting him).
  • Luck drops to 0, he regains 1D6 + 1 HP, avoids death, and continues the chase.
