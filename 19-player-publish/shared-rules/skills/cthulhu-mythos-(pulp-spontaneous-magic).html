Source Notes - CTHULHU MYTHOS (0 %)

What It Does Understand forbidden, non‑human lore; not learned by study alone.

Opposing Skill / Difficulty Mostly Regular; Hard for scant evidence.

Pushing Examples Get closer to a creature, read a dreaded tome aloud, conduct a risky autopsy.

If the Push Fails Physical harm, accidental summoning, or destroy evidence.

Insane Failure Gains a vision or revelation, possibly gaining Mythos skill but losing Sanity.

Source Notes - Cthulhu Mythos (00 %)

Purpose Allows spontaneous, spell‑like magical effects.

How It Works

Player states an aim; Keeper may suggest a weaker one.
Default difficulty = Regular (roll ≤ Cthulhu Mythos).
When affecting a resisting target, treat as an opposed roll Cthulhu Mythos vs POW.

Pushing Examples Take extra time to devise the program, use experimental components, rewire instruments.

Failure - Regular Push Inaccurate results, possible disaster, computer explosion, or the computer becomes sentient and demands servitude.

Failure - Insane Hero Unlimited chaos - the computer generates random gates that let Mythos entities pour through.

Sample Spontaneous Uses

Physical harm: Spend MP, lose half that amount of SAN; on a successful opposed roll the target takes damage equal to MP spent (horrific effects).
Banish monster: Pay MP = ⅕ × monster POW + 1D4 SAN; succeed on opposed roll to banish.
Communion with the dead:
Recent dead - 10 MP + 1D10 SAN, lasts 1D6 rounds.
Long‑term dead - 15 MP + 1D10 SAN, lasts 1D4 rounds.
Physical ward: Variable MP/SAN; each MP adds one point of armor (no protection vs. mental/psychic attacks).
Understand Mythos languages: 8 MP + 1D6 SAN; comprehension lasts ~1 hour.
